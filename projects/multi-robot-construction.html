Multi-Robot Collaborative Assembly

Localization-guided assembly of a timber reciprocal shell

2024 · Collaborative project at Tongji University.

Tutors: Xu Zhen, Zhang Ye, Xiang Wang

We explored how three robots could assemble a timber reciprocal shell. One robot provided localization support without handling boards; two assembly robots used grippers and cameras to manipulate the timber components and build the reciprocal structure together.

02 / LEARNING EXPLORATION

Trajectory Learning Exploration

We explored whether repeated visual demonstrations could support a learned representation of timber assembly motion.

Three Assembly Configurations

Three timber-overlap configurations form the reciprocal shell. Their order and repetition define the assembly sequence. Different groove orientations on the upper and lower faces require each board’s face and direction to be distinguished.

Demonstration Collection & Motion Tracking

We recorded repeated demonstrations of three timber-overlap configurations. Each recording captured one complete assembly action, forming a trajectory sample within its configuration-specific demonstration set. Eight differently colored semicircular markers near the board grooves provided visual features for following motion across frames and distinguishing board faces. Following the same colored marker over time produces a trajectory that represents its movement in the image.

03 / ROBOTIC ASSEMBLY

Localization-Guided Assembly

With the task defined, we used localization to support robot operations. Environment and component information served different roles in the assembly workflow.

A Locate

The environment-localization design uses LiDAR / SLAM for workspace and mobile-base localization. Black-and-white visual markers on the timber boards supported component localization. The colored semicircular markers served a separate purpose: following demonstration motion for trajectory-learning exploration.

Localization Camera View

A camera on the third robot provided visual observations for localization support. This robot did not carry timber boards; the other two robots performed board handling and assembly.

B Coordinate

We assigned different roles to the three robots. One robot provided localization support without carrying timber, while the other two handled and assembled the boards in a shared workspace.

C Manipulate

Each assembly robot used a gripper and camera to handle timber boards. The handling cycle involved grasping a board, moving and aligning it with the target, placing and releasing it, and repeating the process for the next board.
