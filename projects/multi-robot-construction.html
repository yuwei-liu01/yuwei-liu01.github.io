Multi-Robot Collaborative Assembly

Localization-guided assembly of a timber reciprocal shell

Tongji University · 2024 · Team Workshop Project

This team workshop project explored coordinated robotic assembly of a timber reciprocal shell. The final construction approach used environment and component localization to support robot operations. The project also included an initial exploration of learning from recorded demonstrations.

02 / PERCEPTION

Perception & Localization

Localization addressed two distinct needs: locating the robotic setup in its environment and locating timber components for manipulation.

Environment Localization

The workshop diagram connects LiDAR-based SLAM mapping, indoor maps, scan matching, and mobile-base localization. A parallel branch shows point-cloud visualization, an electronic fence, and path planning.
