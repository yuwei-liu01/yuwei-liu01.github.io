Yuwei Liu

M.S. Student in Mechanical Engineering (Robotics), Johns Hopkins University

I am a master’s student in Mechanical Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, pursuing the Robotics track. I work in the ARCADE Lab within the Laboratory for Computational Sensing and Robotics (LCSR), where I contribute to research on learning-based surgical manipulation for robotic cholecystectomy. My work involves surgical perception and the implementation and evaluation of robot learning methods.

My interests include medical robotics, robot learning, and perception, particularly how these capabilities can support robotic assistance. More broadly, I am interested in connecting perception, planning, and control to build robotic systems that work effectively with people.

Previously, I earned my bachelor’s degree in Architecture from Wuhan University of Science and Technology and worked as an Assistant Architect at Central South Architectural Design Institute. My background in computational design and fabrication informs my approach to spatial reasoning, prototyping, and interdisciplinary problem solving.
